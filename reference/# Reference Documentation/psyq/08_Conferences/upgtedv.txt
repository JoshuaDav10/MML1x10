Divide in 2 dimensions or 3 dimensions?




          3 dimensions                                                                     2 dimensions

                         Divide in three dimensions
  • 3 dimensions provides more accuracy
  • Because GTE calculations are performed at high speeds,
    there is no overhead with 3-dimensional division
