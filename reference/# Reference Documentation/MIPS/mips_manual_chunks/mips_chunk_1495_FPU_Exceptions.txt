                   6                  5                  5                 5                  5                   6

            Format:    SUBU rd, rs, rt                                                                                MIPS32

            Purpose:
            To subtract 32-bit integers

            Description: rd ← rs - rt
            The 32-bit word value in GPR rt is subtracted from the 32-bit value in GPR rs and the 32-bit arithmetic result is and
            placed into GPR rd.
