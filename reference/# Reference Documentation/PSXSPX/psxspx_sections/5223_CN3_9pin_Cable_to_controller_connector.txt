CN3 9pin Cable to controller connector
M1 2pin Left Motor with large weight (wire1=red, wire2=black)
M2 2pin Right Motor with small weight (wire1=white, wire2=black)
R1..R28 resistors (plus additional resistors printed on the foil)
C1..C14 capacitors

Analog Buttons

The foil contains printed resistors (large 19K ohm pull-down resistors, and
small 7.6K ohm pull-up resistors). The pull-downs can get shorted by pressing a
conductive piece of rubber against it:

| ____________
'. .' | ____________
'--____--' '. .' | 

There are some oddities: The microprocessor has only two ADC inputs (the PCB
contains analog multiplexors to map the currently selected signals to the ADC
inputs). And, the pull-downs are GNDed via transistors instead of being
directly wired to GND (that is used to share DPAD inputs, and may also reduce
power consumption during inactivity).

| .############. .############.###. .############.###. 

There is only one MUX and one pull-up shared for DPAD LT/RT (and same for
DPAD.UP/DN). Shared access is done by activating only one of the pull-down
transistors (that should work reliably even if it were mechanically possible to
push LT/RT simultaneously).
