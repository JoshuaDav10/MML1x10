004h 2 Sample Rate Pitch (1000h=44100Hz, 0800h=22050Hz, 05CEh=16000Hz)
006h 2 Unknown (7Fh)
008h 4 Unknown (1) (1) (8)
00Ch 4 Unknown (42008Fh) (1FC0001Fh) (40008Fh)
010h 4 Filesize (xxx0h) (xxx0h)
